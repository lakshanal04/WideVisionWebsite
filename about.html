The studio

From Tamil Nadu to the world.

WideVision VFX Studio was established in 2016 to create visual effects for film.

Today, the studio serves movies, short films, and advertising films with a team of more than 30 artists. Our head office is in Chennai, with a branch in Coimbatore.
